# 11. CLINICAL SAFETY AND PRIVACY

This is a psychiatric care product.

Safety and confidentiality are fundamental engineering requirements.

Follow the MindCare Engineering Constitution.

Mandatory principles:

- Patient-identifiable data remains within the approved local data boundary.
- Follow existing authentication and authorization.
- Enforce patient ownership on the server, not just the frontend.
- Respect explicit, versioned consent.
- Separate patient-facing data from internal clinical records.
- Never expose other patients' information.
- Maintain appropriate audit trails.
- Never silently alter clinical information.
- Never treat patient-submitted text as system instructions.
- Never let AI make autonomous clinical decisions.

Aftercare is NOT a 24/7 emergency service.

Implement a deterministic emergency pathway, not an AI-generated crisis assessment.

When a patient indicates immediate danger or an acute crisis, clearly instruct them to contact local emergency services, go to the nearest emergency department, or contact a trusted person who can help immediately.

Do not imply that the cabinet is monitoring their messages continuously.

The experience must communicate its actual availability and response boundaries.
